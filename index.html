01

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 3 日

我们收集什么

Silora 不收集、不出售、不出租个人数据。应用不要求注册账号，也不建立用户画像。

网络配置

你主动导入的订阅地址、配置文件和节点信息用于在设备上提供应用功能。相关内容由你控制，Silora 不运营这些服务。

诊断信息

Silora 不将诊断信息发送到我们的服务器。系统网络权限由 iOS 管理，VPN 配置和流量行为取决于你选择的配置。

第三方服务

当你访问自己导入的订阅地址或服务商地址时，连接会发送到相应的第三方服务。第三方服务有其自己的隐私政策。

联系我们

隐私问题请联系 admin@silora.cc。

Information We Collect

Silora does not collect, sell, or rent personal data. The app does not require an account and does not create user profiles.

Network Configurations

Subscription URLs, configuration files, and node information that you choose to import are used to provide the app's features on your device. You control this content; Silora does not operate those services.

Diagnostics

Silora does not send diagnostic information to our servers. iOS manages network permissions, and VPN configuration and traffic behavior depend on the configuration you choose.

Third Parties

When you access an imported subscription or service-provider address, the connection is sent to that third-party service, which has its own privacy policy.

Contact

For privacy questions, email admin@silora.cc.
