隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 4 日

开发者不收集 VPN 数据

Silora 不要求注册或登录。Silora 与 GRAHAM & ASSOCIATES, INC. 不会从应用接收、收集、出售或出租 VPN 流量、浏览历史、DNS 查询、目标地址、IP 地址、设备标识符或使用行为，也不使用广告、分析、追踪或用户画像服务。

设备本地诊断日志

为供你本人排查连接故障，Silora 会在设备本地短期记录连接诊断元数据。原始本地日志可能包含目标主机名或 IP 地址、端口、所选出站线路和失败原因。日志仅保存在应用本地容器中，不会自动发送给 Silora、开发者或任何分析、广告服务。

当你在应用内查看、复制或导出诊断日志时，目标主机名和 IP 地址会被替换为占位符。日志按应用内说明的生命周期覆盖或删除，并在卸载应用时删除。

第三方订阅服务器和网络提供者

Silora 会访问你主动导入的第三方订阅地址或配置地址。第三方订阅服务器可能获知你的 IP 地址、请求时间、客户端类型以及订阅地址中内嵌的访问凭据。VPN 启用后，网络流量会经过你选择的第三方服务器。这些第三方服务不由 Silora 或 GRAHAM & ASSOCIATES, INC. 拥有、运营或控制，其数据处理适用各自的隐私政策和你与其达成的协议。

本地数据保存

你导入的配置和诊断信息可能保存在设备或 iOS 管理的应用容器中，仅用于提供应用功能和本地排障。Silora 不会把这些内容发送到开发者运营的服务器。

No VPN Data Collection by the Developer

Silora does not require registration or login. Silora and GRAHAM & ASSOCIATES, INC. do not receive, collect, sell, or rent VPN traffic, browsing history, DNS queries, destination addresses, IP addresses, device identifiers, or usage behavior from the app. We do not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or profiling services.

Local Diagnostic Log

For troubleshooting by you, Silora temporarily records connection diagnostic metadata locally on your device. The original local log may contain destination hostnames or IP addresses, ports, selected outbound routes, and failure reasons. It remains only in the app's local container and is never automatically transmitted to Silora, the Developer, or any analytics or advertising service.

When you view, copy, or export the diagnostic log through the app, destination hostnames and IP addresses are replaced with placeholders. Logs are overwritten or removed according to the lifecycle described in the app and are deleted when the app is uninstalled.

Third-Party Subscription Servers and Network Providers

Silora accesses third-party subscription or configuration URLs that you choose to import. A third-party subscription server may observe your IP address, request time, client type, and credentials embedded in the subscription URL. When the VPN is active, network traffic passes through the third-party server you select. These services are not owned, operated, or controlled by Silora or GRAHAM & ASSOCIATES, INC.; their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies and agreements with you.

Local Data Storage

Imported configurations and diagnostic information may be stored on your device or in the app container managed by iOS only to provide app functionality and local troubleshooting. Silora does not send this content to servers operated by the Developer.
