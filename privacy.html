隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

中文

我们不收集个人数据

Silora 不要求注册或登录，不收集、不出售、不出租个人数据，也不使用广告、分析、追踪或用户画像服务。

第三方订阅服务器

Silora 会访问你主动导入的第三方订阅地址或配置地址，以获取配置并提供应用功能。该请求会发送到你选择的第三方服务器。Silora 不拥有、不运营这些服务器，也不会将其内容发送到 Silora 自己的服务器。

VPN 和网络流量

VPN 配置和网络流量由 iOS 和你选择的配置管理。Silora 不保存或分析你的网络流量。

数据保存

你导入的配置可能保存在你的设备或 iOS 管理的应用容器中，用于应用运行。删除应用后，这些本地数据会随系统规则移除。

联系我们

隐私问题请联系 admin@silora.cc。

English

No Personal Data Collection

Silora does not require registration or login. We do not collect, sell, or rent personal data, and we do not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or profiling services.

Third-Party Subscription Servers

Silora accesses third-party subscription or configuration URLs that you choose to import in order to retrieve configuration data and provide app features. Those requests are sent to the third-party servers you select. Silora does not own or operate those servers and does not send their contents to Silora-operated servers.

VPN and Network Traffic

VPN configuration and network traffic are managed by iOS and the configuration you choose. Silora does not store or analyze your network traffic.

Data Storage

Imported configurations may be stored on your device or in the app container managed by iOS to provide app functionality. Local app data is removed according to iOS rules when the app is deleted.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact admin@silora.cc.
